Variable Selection for Financial Dataset

I selected the following variables for my financial data analysis:

1. Segment
    Reason: To compare financial performance across different business segments.
2. Product
    Reason: To analyze sales and profit for different products.
3. Units Sold
    Reason: To understand how the number of units sold affects sales and profit.
4. Sale Price
    Reason: To examine the relationship between selling price and financial performance.
5. Gross Sales
    Reason: To analyze total sales before deductions.
6. COGS
    Reason: To understand the cost of goods sold and its relationship with profit.
7. Profit
    Reason: To analyze the overall profitability of the business.

These variables were selected because they are directly related to sales, costs, and profitability in the financial dataset.
